# ACEKOR Calculator: Play Store listing

**App name (30):** ACEKOR Calculator

**Short description (80):** Score calculator for teachers: search student, type score, save. Works offline.

## Full description

ACEKOR Calculator is the quickest way for college instructors to record quiz, recitation and examination scores.

OPEN THE APP → SEARCH STUDENT → ENTER SCORE → SAVE.

• Big, easy calculator: the score and percentage appear in large numbers (42/50 → 84%).
• Find any student in seconds by first name, last name or student ID, even with partial names.
• One green button saves the score, and the next student in your class list comes up automatically.
• Quizzes, recitations and examinations with your own totals (not limited to 50 points).
• Weighted grades with your own percentages (e.g. Quizzes 30%, Recitation 20%, Midterm 20%, Final 20%, Practical 10%) and grade equivalents (1.00–5.00 or your own scale).
• Class records, accumulated recitation scores, class averages and a change history of every score.
• Printable PDF reports and real Excel (.xlsx) workbooks with formulas.
• Import your class list from Excel or CSV.
• A separate full calculator (+ − × ÷ %) with history.
• Works without internet. Your students' records stay on your phone, encrypted.
• Optional PIN or fingerprint lock and password-protected backups.
• Large text and high contrast, made for comfortable use at any age.
