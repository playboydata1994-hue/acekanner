# ACEkor Grading Sheet: closed-test testers

Google Play requires **at least 12 testers** to stay in the closed test for **14 days in a row**
before the app can be published. Invite 15 or more colleagues in case someone drops out.

## 1. Make the tester group (owner, 5 minutes)

Testers join a Google Group; the group is then added to the closed test in Play Console, so
nobody's email has to be typed into Play Console.

1. Open **groups.google.com** with the developer Google account → **Create group**.
2. **Name:** `ACEkor testers`. **Group email:** `acekor-testers` (if taken, try
   `acekor-testers-nye94`). → **Next**.
3. **Privacy settings:**
   - Who can search for group: **Anyone on the web**.
   - Who can join group: **Anyone can join**.
   - Who can view conversations: **Group members**.
   - Who can post: **Group managers**.
   - Who can view members: **Group managers** (testers don't see each other's emails).
4. Skip adding members → **Create group**.
5. Copy the group's link, e.g. `https://groups.google.com/g/acekor-testers`, and put it in
   message 1 below.

## 2. Message 1: send now (join the group)

> Hi! I made an Android app for us instructors: **ACEkor Grading Sheet**. You search a student,
> type the score on a big keypad, and it saves the score and percentage (e.g. 42/50 → 84%) to your
> class record. It also does weighted grades, PDF/Excel reports, and works offline.
>
> Before it goes on the Play Store, Google requires at least 12 people to test it for 14 days.
> Could you help? It's free, and you can use it for your real classes.
>
> **What you need:** an Android phone (Android 7 or newer) and the Gmail account you use in the
> Play Store.
>
> **Step 1 (now):** open this link with that Gmail account and tap **Join group**:
> GROUP_LINK
>
> In a few days I'll send you the Play Store link to install it. Thank you!

## 3. Message 2: send when the closed test is live (install)

> The ACEkor Grading Sheet test is ready. Thank you for joining!
>
> 1. On your phone, open this link (with the same Gmail account) and tap **Become a tester**:
>    OPT_IN_LINK
> 2. Tap **Download it on Google Play** and install **ACEkor Grading Sheet**.
> 3. Open it and tap **Try with a sample class**, or add your own class. Tap **Start free
>    trial** and sign in with Google once. Everything is unlocked during the test.
> 4. **Please keep the app installed and stay in the test for at least 14 days**, and open it every
>    few days. Google counts this.
>
> Your students' names and scores stay on your phone; they are never uploaded.
>
> If something is wrong or confusing, tell me, or use **Send feedback** on the app's Play Store
> page. Screenshots help a lot!

## 4. Owner checklist during the test

- Count the members on **groups.google.com → ACEkor testers → Members**: 12 or more.
- Play Console → **Closed testing → Testers**: the group email is added, and the opt-in link is
  copied into message 2.
- Day 1 is the day 12 testers have opted in. Keep a note of the date; day 15 is the earliest day
  to **Apply for production**.
