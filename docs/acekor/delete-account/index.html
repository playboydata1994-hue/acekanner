Delete your ACEKOR account

ACEKOR Calculator keeps a small licensing account on our server when you sign in with Google for the free trial or a subscription (see the Privacy Policy). Your student records are never on our server; they stay on your phone.

In the app

  1. Cancel any active subscription first in Google Play (Play Store › Profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions).
  2. Open ACEKOR › Menu › Plan (or Settings › Subscription).
  3. Tap Delete licensing account and confirm.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email espinosanoli1994@gmail.com from the Google account you used, with the subject “Delete my ACEKOR account”. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your account, phone IDs, subscription records and trial record.
  • Kept: a one-way code made from your Google account ID with your trial dates, so the free trial cannot be started again; Google Play keeps its own purchase records under its policies; sign-in logs expire after 90 days.
  • Not affected: records on your phone. Delete them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.
  • If you also use ACEkanner with the same Google account, your ACEkanner account is kept.
