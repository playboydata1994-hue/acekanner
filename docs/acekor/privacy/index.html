ACEkor Grading Sheet Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026
App: ACEkor Grading Sheet (Android, package com.acekor.calculator)
Developer: NYE94, Philippines
Contact / Data Protection Officer: espinosanoli1994@gmail.com

ACEkor Grading Sheet helps college instructors compute and record quiz, recitation and examination scores. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept, and your rights under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

The short version

  • Your students' information never leaves your phone. Names, student IDs, scores, grades and reports are stored only on your phone, encrypted. We do not receive them.
  • For the free trial and the PRO/MAX subscriptions you sign in with Google once. Our server then keeps your Google account identity, a random ID for each phone, your trial dates and your subscription status.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.

1. Information that stays on your phone

ACEkor stores the following only on your phone, in a database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher); the key is kept in the Android Keystore: your instructor and school details; sections; students (names, student IDs, course, year level, section); subjects, activities, scores, grades and their change history; settings; and the mini-calculator history.

This information is not uploaded to our server or to any cloud service. The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup so it is not copied to Google Drive without you. It leaves your phone only when you share or save a report or a backup file, and you choose where it goes. Backup files are encrypted with a password you choose.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it from your phone unless you made a backup.

2. Information our server receives

InformationWhy
Google account ID, email address and name (from Google Sign-In)To create and identify your ACEkor account
An account ID created by our serverTo link your phones, trial and subscription
An install ID for each phone (a random ID made by the app, not a hardware ID) and the app versionTo bind plan information to your phone and to support you
Trial start and end datesTo give the 7-day free trial once per Google account
Google Play subscription details: purchase token, product (PRO or MAX), status, expiry date, order IDTo confirm and renew your subscription with Google Play
Sign-in and technical logs of our hosting and sign-in services (time, email used, IP address, device type)Security and troubleshooting

We never receive student names, student IDs, scores or grades. We do not collect your location, contacts, files, payment card details (Google Play handles payment) or advertising ID.

3. How we use it

To provide your trial and subscription; to verify purchases, renewals, cancellations and refunds with Google Play; to prevent repeated free trials; to answer support requests; and to meet legal obligations. Under RA 10173 we process this information because it is necessary for the service you asked for, with your consent, and for our legitimate interest in preventing abuse.

4. Reminders

The app schedules reminders on your phone (5 days and, if you keep it on, 1 day before your subscription ends, and 1 day before the trial ends). They are created and shown by the phone; nothing is sent to us.

5. Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — database and server functions, hosted in Singapore.
  • Google — Google Sign-In, Google Play Billing and the Google Play Developer API.

We do not sell or rent your information and share it with no one else unless the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information is kept while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account we delete your account, phones and subscription records. We keep only a one-way code made from your Google account ID together with your trial dates, so the free trial cannot be taken again by deleting and re-creating the account. The code cannot identify you by itself.
  • Sign-in logs are deleted after 90 days.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your information, object to its processing, or file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). You can delete your account in the app (Settings › Subscription › Delete licensing account) or as described on the account deletion page.

8. Your students' data

As the instructor, you decide what student information to keep and are its personal information controller. ACEkor helps you protect it with encryption, an optional PIN or fingerprint lock, password-protected backups and no automatic uploads.

9. Children

ACEkor is made for instructors, not for students, and is not directed to children.

10. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date.
