Delete your ACEkanner account

App: ACEkanner (Android) · Developer: NYE94

This page explains how to delete your ACEkanner account and what happens to your information. You can do it in the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ACEkanner.
  2. Go to Settings → Plan and account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted from our server right away.

Option 2 — By email (without the app)

Send an email to espinosanoli1994@gmail.com:

  • Subject: Delete my ACEkanner account
  • From: the Google account (Gmail) you used to sign in to ACEkanner, so we know the request is yours.

We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

From the ACEkanner server:

  • your account and your Google sign-in record (email address, name, profile-picture link and Google account ID)
  • the phones linked to your account (install IDs and app versions)
  • your scan records (the one-way codes used to count scans)
  • your Google Play subscription records kept by our server

What is kept

  • A one-way code made from your Google account ID, and the number of free scans you had used. This stops the free plan from being reset by deleting and re-creating an account. The code cannot be turned back into your email or name. It is kept for as long as ACEkanner offers a free plan.
  • Sign-in logs (the time, email address and IP address of each sign-in). They are deleted automatically 90 days after the sign-in.
  • Google Play purchase records, which Google keeps under its own policies.

Before you delete

  • Deleting your account does not cancel your Premium subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play Store app: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → ACEkanner → Cancel subscription.
  • Free scans you already used are not restored.
  • Your examinations, students and results stay on your phone. They were never on our server. To delete them, delete the examinations in the app, or uninstall ACEkanner (or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → ACEkanner → Storage → Clear storage).
