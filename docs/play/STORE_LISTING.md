### Full description (2986 / 4000)

```text
Check your students' shaded answer sheets with your phone camera. ACEkanner reads each paper in seconds, scores it with your answer key, and shows which questions your class missed most. Made for teachers and instructors in the Philippines.

PRINT YOUR ANSWER SHEETS
• 50, 75 or 100 questions, choices A to E
• Up to 5 exam sets (Set A to Set E), each with its own answer key
• Shade-the-circle design similar to board exam answer sheets
• Student number boxes, so each paper is matched to the right student
• Add your school logo and name (optional)
• Print or share the sheets as PDF

SCAN AND CHECK
• Point the camera at the sheet. ACEkanner finds the page, reads the shaded circles and checks them right away
• A result card shows the student and score after each sheet: confirm and go on to the next, or open Review
• Quick check from Home: scan any ACEkanner sheet, even an old one, to see its score without saving
• Blank, unclear and double-shaded answers are flagged for you to review
• When a photo cannot be read, the app tells you why and what to fix
• Correct a reading yourself; the score updates and the change is recorded
• Mark all flagged and double-shaded answers wrong in one tap
• Save a paper even when the student is not identified, and choose the student later
• Re-scanning a paper you already saved does not use another scan

ANSWER KEYS AND SCORING
• Separate answer key for each exam set
• Use fewer questions than the sheet has, for example 90 on a 100-item sheet
• Set points per question and optional deductions for wrong answers
• Change the key later and all saved papers are re-scored

CLASSES AND STUDENTS
• Organise sections and student lists
• Import your class list from Excel (.xlsx) or CSV
• Or type the students in, or save an empty Excel class list to fill in

RESULTS
• Class summary and score distribution
• Most missed questions per exam set, with the share of students who got each one right and a discrimination index to spot unclear questions
• Open any paper to see the scanned sheet and each answer

WORKS OFFLINE, KEEPS DATA ON YOUR PHONE
• Scanning, checking and results work without internet
• Student names, numbers, answers and scores stay on your phone, in an encrypted database. They are not uploaded to our server
• No ads

FREE AND PREMIUM
• Free: 100 scans in total, with all features
• ACEkanner Premium: unlimited scans with a yearly subscription through Google Play. Cancel any time in Google Play
• A scan is counted only when a student's result is saved
• Sign in with Google once to keep your scan count and Premium when you change phones. Internet is needed to sign in, to buy Premium, and to confirm your plan at least once every 30 days

TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
Print at actual size on white paper, use a dark pencil or pen, and scan in good light with the whole sheet inside the frame.

ACEkanner follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Privacy policy: https://playboydata1994-hue.github.io/acekanner/privacy/
```
