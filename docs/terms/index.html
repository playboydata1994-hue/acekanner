4. Free plan

  • Each account gets 100 free scans in total (not per month).
  • A scan is counted when a student's result is saved, or when a paper is checked with Scan on the Home screen (quick check). Each student's paper is counted once per examination: scanning, checking or saving it again is not counted. Blurry or rejected scans are not counted. A quick check of a paper whose student is not identified is counted each time.
  • Free scans are used on one phone at a time.
  • Deleting and re-creating an account does not give new free scans.

You are responsible for reviewing the results and for the final grades you give. Check flagged items, and check any result that looks wrong against the paper before you release grades. Use the ACEkanner answer sheets printed from the app, at actual size.
